## Outreach Email Templates

**Subject:** Free AME exam prep for your [School Name] students

```

No strings attached — just a useful resource for your students to practice Transport Canada–style questions alongside their coursework.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Quick Tips

1. **Always personalize** the first line of each email
2. **Follow up** after 5-7 days if no response
3. **Track** outreach in a simple spreadsheet (name, school, email sent, follow-up, response)
4. **Prioritize** schools first — they have the most to gain and the best conversion
5. **BCIT, NAIT, Sault College** are the largest AME programs in Canada — start there
