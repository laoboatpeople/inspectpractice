## Technical SEO Recommendations

### Priority — High
1. **Blog category/tag taxonomy:** Add topic categories (Licensing, Exams, Career, Technology, Reference) as URL prefixes or filterable taxonomy. Already visually present on blog cards but not reflected in URL structure or dedicated landing pages.
2. **Internal linking:** Blog posts do not interlink to each other or to product pages (/pricing, /faq). Each post should link to at least 2-3 other relevant posts and 1 product page. Specifically, /blog/ame-exam-study-guide should link to /blog/cars-exam-guide, /blog/tp14038e-syllabus, and /pricing.
3. **Blog pagination:** If blog grows beyond ~12 posts, add pagination or load-more with proper `rel="next"/"prev"`.
4. **Blog article schema:** Add `Article` or `BlogPosting` schema (in addition to BreadcrumbList) for richer SERP snippets — currently only BreadcrumbList is present on blog pages.

### Priority — Medium
5. **Sitemap updates:** `/study-checklist` is listed as "coming soon" — ensure it's in the sitemap once published. Consider adding a `<lastmod>` frequency trigger for blog pages when updated.
6. **Related posts section:** Add a "You Might Also Like" section at the bottom of each blog post to reduce bounce rate and increase pageviews.
7. **Table of contents:** Long-form posts (glossary at 15 min, study guide at 13 min) would benefit from a sticky table of contents with anchor links for better user experience and featured snippet targeting.
8. **Image optimization:** Blog posts appear to use no in-content images, diagrams, or infographics. Adding relevant visuals (AME license flowchart, exam subject diagram, salary comparison chart) would improve engagement and image search traffic.

### Priority — Low
9. **French language content:** The `inLanguage` schema includes "fr" but no French pages exist. Consider French versions for top 3 blog posts if targeting Quebec AME candidates.
10. **FAQ schema on individual blog posts:** Add FAQ structured data to posts that answer common questions (e.g., "How long does it take to get an AME license?").
11. **Author bylines:** Adding author attribution (even pseudonymous) with author schema can improve EEAT signals.
